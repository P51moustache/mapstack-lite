# MapStack Lite

Pick a landscape, press Create, and a whole map appears in your Godot 3D view: terrain, ground, water, trees,
rocks, grass and daylight. MapStack Lite is the small installer for it. It adds one dock that installs MapStack Free
into your project, turns it on, and shows what MapStack Pro adds.

- Trailer: https://youtu.be/M5GOXx_ELGY
- MapStack Free: https://thepixelgoblin.itch.io/mapstack
- MapStack Pro: https://thepixelgoblin.itch.io/mapstack-pro

## Use it

1. Install MapStack Lite from the Asset Library and turn it on (Project > Project Settings > Plugins).
2. In the MapStack Lite dock, press **Install MapStack Free** (or **Get MapStack Free**, which opens its page).
3. Open the MapStack dock, pick a landscape and press **Create landscape**.

Needs Godot 4.4 or newer.
